Privacy policy

KLOZIO, a B2B sales simulation game, on the web and on Google Play. Publisher: Squadra Nostra Games.

In short

  • No account, email or name asked for. KLOZIO stores no email address.
  • No advertising, no cookies, no data sold or shared.
  • Your progress stays on your device (the browser's local storage).

Audience measurement: 5 events, no identifier

The game sends 5 events, added to daily counters:

  • visit: a visit;
  • deal_start: a deal started;
  • deal_3: a 3rd deal played;
  • deal_end: a deal finished, with the deal's identifier, its result (signed, delayed, lost) and its number of senior instincts (0 to 5), counted per deal to show where you stand against other players;
  • share: a share.

Only the day, the edition, the interface language and these counters are kept. No identifier, no cookie, no IP address, no time of day.

Access codes: a random device identifier

Without a code, nothing else is sent. If you enter an access code, your device sends a random identifier (device_id), drawn by the game and linked to no other data. It is kept with the code, along with the day of first and last use, to limit the number of devices per code.

Team code (KLOZIO Team): in addition, the nickname you choose and, for each finished deal, its 5 grades, its result and its day (no time), for the team ranking and the manager dashboard. Nothing else.

The IP address is read when a code is entered, to limit attempts. It is neither written nor kept: a salted fingerprint stays in memory for an hour at most.

Purchases on the website

Payment is handled by Stripe, under its own privacy policy. KLOZIO keeps only the payment session identifier and the code it produced: no email, no name, no amount. No purchases in the Google Play app.

Hosting, deletion, contact

Hosting: GitHub Pages (the game), Cloudflare (measurement and codes).

Deletion: email us with your code and the devices and team data linked to it are erased.
